# CINEMORA — Product Vision & Voice Guide

## 1. Product Overview
CINEMORA is an independent, real-time movie ticket booking platform. It connects moviegoers directly to theater auditorium layouts, live showtimes, and digital gate passes without box office queues or hidden checkout fees.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Discerning Film Lovers**: Care about screening formats (IMAX 70mm, 4K Laser, Dolby Atmos), audio calibration, and original aspect ratios.
- **Weekend Theatergoers**: Want quick showtime discovery, clear seating availability, and frictionless mobile check-in.
- **Theater Staff & Gate Stewards**: Need instantaneous QR code scanning at auditorium entrances to verify bookings offline or online.

## 3. Brand Voice & Humanized Writing Rules
- **Direct and Grounded**: State exactly what the product does. Never invent fake statistics ("500K+ users", "99.99% magic").
- **Zero AI Clichés**: Ban words like *delve, elevate, embark, unleash, seamless, tapestry, testament, premier destination, revolutionized*.
- **No Staged Triads**: Stop writing triplets of adjectives or features just for rhythm.
- **No Hollow Closers**: Ban sentences like *"Ready to embark on your cinematic journey? Book now!"*
- **Action-Oriented Microcopy**: Buttons say what happens next: *"Choose Seats"*, *"Confirm & Pay \$24.00"*, *"Download Digital Pass"*.

## 4. Core User Jobs to Be Done
1. **Find What's Playing**: Browse today's schedule, filter by format (IMAX, 4K Laser, Dolby Atmos) and language.
2. **Select Exact Seats**: Interactive theater matrix with instant seat hold and zero double-booking risk.
3. **Walk In With QR**: Skip ticket counters; present the high-contrast digital QR pass at the gate.
4. **Manage & Cancel**: Transparent cancellation windows with instant refund calculations.
